    Structure market

    Search lists structures you can dock at. Each result is checked by reading one orders page (up to 20 per search); answers are remembered for 24 hours. "No market" means ESI refused the market read, most likely because the structure has no market service installed. Hover the badge to see what ESI answered.

      ESI cannot list every market structure: search by name or enter an id. ESI refuses the read (403 or 404) when the structure has no market service or the character has no market access.

      Watched structures

      While the app runs, the orders of watched structures are collected periodically (see --collect-interval) and changes between collections are stored. Use Watch on a search result that has a market.
